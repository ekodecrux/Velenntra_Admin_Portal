About Biofuels 2027 | Hybrid Event

Taking place from September 9–11, 2027, in Valencia, Spain, and Online, the 4th Edition of Global Conference on Biofuels and Bioenergy (Biofuels 2027) will provide a global stage for examining how biological resources can be converted into clean, reliable, and affordable energy. Centered on the theme “Advancing Biofuels and Bioenergy for a Sustainable Future”, the hybrid conference highlights the research that is moving bio-based fuels from the laboratory to commercial scale.

The event is designed for bioenergy researchers, biochemical and chemical engineers, microbiologists, biotechnologists, agronomists, energy economists, biorefinery operators, technology developers, investors, policymakers, academicians, and students. By connecting those who grow the feedstock, those who engineer the conversion, and those who shape energy policy, Biofuels 2027 encourages collaborations that address the full bioenergy value chain.

Scientific sessions will address biomass feedstocks and energy crops, bioethanol and biodiesel production, advanced and cellulosic biofuels, algal biofuels, biogas and biomethane, biohydrogen, sustainable aviation fuels, thermochemical and biochemical conversion, biorefinery design, enzyme and microbial engineering, waste-to-energy pathways, life cycle and techno-economic assessment, bioenergy policy, and the role of bio-based fuels in a circular bioeconomy.

The program brings together keynote lectures, plenary sessions, oral and poster presentations, panel discussions, young researcher forums, exhibitions, and networking sessions that give every participant the chance to present findings and learn from peers. Join us in Valencia, Spain, or virtually to share your work, build new partnerships, and contribute to a cleaner and more resilient energy future.

3rd Edition of Global Conference on Biofuels and Bioenergy 2026 Report

Magnus Group is delighted to announce the successful conclusion of the 3rd Edition of Global Conference on Biofuels and Bioenergy (Biofuels 2026), held from September 17-19, 2026, in Rome, Italy and Online, as a Hybrid Event.

With the theme “Biofuels & Bioenergy: Progress, Prospects, and Practice,” Biofuels 2026 provided a dynamic platform for researchers, scientists, academics, industry professionals, and experts from around the world to exchange knowledge and present recent developments in the field of biofuels and bioenergy. The conference featured engaging discussions on emerging technologies, innovative production pathways, sustainable feedstocks, biomass utilization, and practical approaches for advancing renewable and low-carbon energy solutions.

Over three dynamic days, participants engaged in thought-provoking keynote sessions, interactive panel discussions, and informative oral and poster presentations. The event fostered meaningful scientific exchange, knowledge sharing, and collaboration, highlighting innovative research and emerging opportunities shaping the future of biofuels and sustainable bioenergy.

We extend our sincere appreciation to all Keynote Speakers, Session Chairs, Speakers, Poster Presenters and Delegates for their valuable contributions and active participation, which made Biofuels 2026 a resounding success.
